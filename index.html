What ProManaged is

A small studio for people who were quoted enterprise prices for an ordinary problem.

Most technology work does not need a department. It needs someone who will listen properly, say plainly what a thing will cost, and then actually deliver it.

That is the whole company. We take on software, hardware and connectivity work for individuals, founders, families and small teams — and we stay reachable long after the invoice is settled.

You talk to the builder

The person scoping your project is the person writing the code or placing the order. Nothing gets lost being relayed.

Scoped to your budget

We work to the number you have, not the one we would prefer. If it will not stretch far enough, we say so and suggest what to drop.

We stay after launch

Delivery is where the real questions start. Support and iteration are part of the work, not a separate contract you discover later.

How we work

Four steps, and you always know which one you are in.

  1. 01

    Listen

    You describe the problem in plain words. No brief required, no vocabulary test. We ask until we genuinely understand what is going wrong.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We come back with a scope, a realistic timeline and an honest cost — including whether we are the right people at all.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    We build, source or install, showing progress as we go. Nothing is unveiled at the end for the first time.

  4. 04

    Stay close

    Questions after launch are part of the relationship. We stay reachable while the work settles into real life.
