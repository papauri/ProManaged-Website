Sound familiar?

Enquiries by phone, bookings in a notebook, records in three chats.

Guests ring to ask what is free. Someone writes it down. A second person promises the same dates over WhatsApp, and nobody can see the month at a glance.

None of it needs a big system. It needs the right first step — usually a website that answers the questions, then bookings and records once they are worth it.

What we build

Three things a property usually needs.

Take one, or build up to all three over time. It is the same team either way, and nothing is built before it is worth building.

A website for the property

  • Photos, rooms or units, location and the questions guests always ask
  • An enquiry form that lands in your inbox
  • Content you can update yourself

Bookings and availability

Guests see what is free and book it themselves, against live availability — the same kind of booking engine we delivered for two Malawian hotels.

Every booking lands in one place the moment it is made, instead of a diary.

A system for your records

Tenants, rent, maintenance, the paperwork that lives in a notebook and three chats today — software built around how you actually run the property, not a template you have to bend to.

Someone to call afterwards

When something looks wrong, one person already knows the setup. Support and changes after launch are part of the work, not a separate contract you discover later.

Bookings we have already built.

How it works

From how you run it to what you need.
