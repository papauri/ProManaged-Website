Sound familiar?

Sold out everywhere, or it will not ship to you.

The console is out of stock. The retailer does not deliver to your country. The card gets declined, and the shipping quote turns up after you have already paid.

We take that whole chain off your hands: one person who finds the gear, checks it is right for what you play, quotes one total and has it ready for you to collect, or brings it to your doorstep.

What we source

Everything a setup needs.

Rigs, consoles, screens and the gear around them — consumer and enthusiast kit from trusted suppliers. If what you want is not listed, ask anyway; the answer is usually yes.

Gaming PCs

Prebuilt or custom rigs, specified against the games you actually play and the resolution you play them at — not the most expensive parts list.

Consoles & controllers

Current-generation consoles and the controllers, chargers and spare pads that sell out everywhere first.

Monitors & displays

High-refresh panels for competitive play and big screens for the couch — matched to the machine driving them.

Headsets, mics & capture

Headsets, streaming microphones, webcams and capture cards for going live at the weekend.

Keyboards, mice & desk gear

Mechanical keyboards, switches, keycaps, mice, mats and the chair-side bits that finish a setup.

Upgrades, storage & parts

Graphics cards, memory, SSDs and cooling — specified against the machine you already own, because an upgrade is often far cheaper than a new rig.

How it works

Three steps, one person to message.

  1. 01

    Tell us the setup

    A model, a link, or just what you play and a budget. We come back with options and an honest recommendation — including when the cheaper one is the better buy.

  2. 02

    One total, then we order

    Gear, freight and handling quoted as one figure before anything is committed, with payment arranged in a way that works where you are.

  3. 03

    Collect it, or we bring it

    Ready for collection locally, or we arrange it to your doorstep — with updates along the way. Want help getting it running? We can walk you through setup over a call.
