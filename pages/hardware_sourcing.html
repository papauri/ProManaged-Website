The awkward part

The thing you want is always out of stock, or will not ship here.

The retailer does not deliver to you. The card gets declined. The shipping quote arrives after you have already paid, and nobody can tell you what the final cost will be.

We take that entire chain off your hands and give you one number and one point of contact instead — whether it is a work laptop or the headset you have been trying to buy for a month.

How it works

Three steps, one point of contact.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you need

    A model, a use case, or just a budget and a rough idea. We come back with options and an honest recommendation — including when the cheaper one is the better buy.

  2. 02

    We source and order

    We buy from trusted suppliers, confirm the full total including shipping before anything is committed, and arrange payment in a way that works where you are.

  3. 03

    We deliver it to you

    Shipping, paperwork and safe delivery to your door — with updates along the way and somebody to call if anything goes sideways.

What we source

If it plugs in, we can probably get it.

Everyday computers, gaming gear and the accessories around them. This is consumer and enthusiast kit for individuals, families and small teams — not data-centre hardware. If what you want is not listed, ask anyway; the answer is usually yes.

Laptops & desktops

Budget-friendly machines for study, admin and working from home, through to the fast ones people buy once and keep for years.

Gaming PCs & consoles

Prebuilt and custom rigs, current-generation consoles, and the controllers, chargers and spare pads that sell out everywhere first.

Monitors & displays

High-refresh gaming panels, colour-accurate screens for editing, and the second monitor that quietly doubles what a day gets through.

Mice, keyboards & desk gear

Wireless and wired mice, mechanical keyboards, switches, keycaps, desk mats and the wrist support you should have bought first.

Headsets, mics & capture

Gaming headsets, streaming microphones, webcams, capture cards and lighting — whether you are on calls all day or going live at the weekend.

Upgrades, storage & accessories

Graphics cards, memory, SSDs, cooling, cables, docks and chargers — the parts that make an upgrade far cheaper than a replacement, specified against the machine you already own rather than sold as a bundle.
