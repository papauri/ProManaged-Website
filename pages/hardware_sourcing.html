The awkward part

Buying good equipment across a border is a second job.

The supplier will not ship to you. The card gets declined. The shipping quote arrives after you have already paid, and nobody can tell you what the final cost will be.

We take that entire chain off your hands and give you one number and one point of contact instead.

How it works

Three steps, one point of contact.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you need

    A model, a use case, or just a budget and a rough idea. We come back with options and an honest recommendation — including when the cheaper one is the better buy.

  2. 02

    We source and order

    We buy from trusted suppliers, confirm the full total including shipping before anything is committed, and arrange payment in a way that works where you are.

  3. 03

    We deliver it to you

    Shipping, paperwork and safe delivery to your door — with updates along the way and somebody to call if anything goes sideways.
