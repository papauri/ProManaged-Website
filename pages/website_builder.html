Why this exists

Most website quotes are answering a question nobody asked.

You ask for a website and get back a price, without ever being asked what it has to do. So you either pay for a shop you will never open, or you get five pages that cannot take a booking.

Start from the job instead. Say what it needs to do, add only that, and see the shape of it before anyone quotes you.
