Questions

Frequently asked.

Select a question to read the answer.

Do you only build websites, or web apps and SaaS too?
Web apps and SaaS platforms are most of what we build today. We still build straightforward business websites when that is the right fit — just tell us what you are trying to achieve.
What is the difference between a website and a web app?
A website mainly presents information. A web app lets people log in and do things — book appointments, manage data, track orders. Most businesses eventually need both.
Do I need to know what I want built before contacting you?
No. Most projects start as a rough idea or a problem you are trying to solve — we help you turn that into a clear scope during the discovery step.
How long does a project take?
It depends entirely on scope. A simple website is a matter of weeks; a full web app or SaaS platform takes considerably longer. You get a realistic timeline in writing after discovery, before any commitment.
How do payments work?
We use flexible payment arrangements agreed up front, and support local-friendly payment options so you are not forced through awkward international transfers.
Do you handle hosting and maintenance after launch?
Yes. We set up hosting and infrastructure as part of the build, and offer ongoing maintenance if you would rather we handle updates.
Will it be secure?
Yes. We follow standard security practices for authentication, data handling and hosting, and use SSL/TLS across every project.
