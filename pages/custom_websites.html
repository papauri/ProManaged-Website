How It Works: Step-by-Step

Step 1: Free Consultation

We discuss your goals, budget, and what type of website fits your needs. Whether you need a business site, an online store, or a portfolio, we’ll help you figure it out.

Step 2: Design & Development

Our designers create a mockup tailored to your brand. Once approved, we build your website, ensuring it’s fast, responsive, and user-friendly.

Step 3: Hosting & Domain

We guide you in purchasing a domain name (yourbusiness.com) and hosting (the online server for your site). Don’t worry—we’ll handle the technical parts.

Step 4: Launch & Training

We launch your website and provide training so you can manage content. If you'd rather focus on your business, we offer maintenance packages.

What is a domain name and why do I need one?
A domain name is your website's unique address on the internet. It's what people type to find your website (e.g., yourbusiness.com). Without it, your website won't be accessible online.
