Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our services

Do you only build websites, or web apps and SaaS too?
Web apps and SaaS platforms are most of what we build today. We still build straightforward business websites when that's the right fit — just tell us what you're trying to achieve.
What's the difference between a website and a web app?
A website mainly presents information. A web app lets users log in and do things — book appointments, manage data, track orders. Most businesses eventually need both.
Do I need to know what I want built before contacting you?
No. Most projects start as a rough idea or a problem you're trying to solve — we help you turn that into a clear scope during the discovery step.
How long does a project take?
It depends on scope. A simple website can take 1-2 weeks, while a full web app or SaaS platform typically takes 4-8 weeks or more.
What's the cost?
It depends on complexity — see the pricing ranges above for a starting point. We'll give you a firm quote after the discovery step.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes, we offer flexible payment plans, and you can pay in your local currency including Malawian Kwacha.
Do you handle hosting and maintenance after launch?
Yes. We set up hosting and infrastructure as part of the build, and offer ongoing maintenance packages if you'd rather we handle updates.
Will it be secure?
Yes. We follow standard security practices for authentication, data handling, and hosting, and use SSL/TLS across every project.
