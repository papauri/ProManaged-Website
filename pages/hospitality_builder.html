Why this exists

Every property runs differently. Most software pretends otherwise.

A twelve-room lodge taking bookings on WhatsApp does not have the same problem as a hotel with a reception desk and a housekeeping rota — but they are usually sold the same thing.

So start from your property instead. Describe how it works, add what your operation actually needs, and see what the system would look like before anyone quotes you for it.
