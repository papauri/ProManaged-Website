Who we help

Three kinds of people keep finding us.

You do not need an IT department, a procurement process or a technical background. Most of our clients have none of the three.

Individuals and families

Buying a first laptop, a work machine or a gaming rig — and wanting somebody honest to check the specification and handle the shipping before any money changes hands.

Small teams and organisations

Outgrowing spreadsheets and shared inboxes, needing a real system, a network that holds up, or equipment that arrives without a logistics headache.

Founders with an idea

Turning a rough concept into a first working product, then into something that can carry paying users without being rebuilt from scratch a year later.

The method

Listen, plan, deliver, support.

The same four movements whether we are writing software, buying equipment or running cable. Only the middle changes.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We ask what is actually going wrong, who it affects and what has already been tried. Often the request that arrives is not the problem worth solving.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A written scope, a realistic timeline and a cost — including the parts we would leave out, and whether the budget is better spent elsewhere.

  3. 03

    Build, source or connect

    The work itself, with progress visible as it happens. Nothing gets unveiled at the end for the first time.

  4. 04

    Support

    We stay reachable for the questions, changes and fixes that only surface once something is genuinely in daily use.

What we take on

The work, in more detail.

Custom web apps and internal tools

Booking systems, dashboards, order and inventory trackers, and the integrations that connect them to whatever you already use. Built around your workflow instead of forcing your workflow around a product.

SaaS platforms

Multi-tenant products from a first version through accounts, subscriptions and scale.

Business websites

Fast, responsive sites with enquiry and booking flows, and a content setup you can manage yourself.

Hardware sourcing

Equipment specified, bought from trusted suppliers, then shipped and delivered to you.

Networks and connectivity

Satellite and fibre, WiFi, cabling, secure remote access, and the maintenance that keeps it steady.

Remote support, wherever you are

Troubleshooting, upgrades and second opinions. Most things get resolved the same day over a call and a screen share — and when it genuinely needs hands on hardware, we arrange that instead of pretending otherwise.

What to expect

The promises we can actually keep.

We will tell you when to spend less

If a cheaper machine does the job, or the feature you asked for is not worth building yet, that is what we will say. It costs us a little now and saves the relationship later.

We will tell you when we are not the right fit

We are a small team and we take on what we can genuinely finish. A referral or an honest no is more useful to you than an over-committed yes.

You will always know the cost before we start

Scope and price go in writing after the planning step. Where something genuinely cannot be fixed up front — international shipping, for instance — we say so and show the range.

We will still be here afterwards

Support is part of the work, not a separate contract you find out about later. You will have a person to contact, not a queue.
