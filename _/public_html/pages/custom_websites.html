Your Custom Website Journey

Looking for a website but not sure where to start? Don't worry—we’ll walk you through every step, from understanding what you need to launching a stunning, professional website.

How It Works: Step-by-Step

Step 1: Free Consultation

We discuss your goals, budget, and what type of website fits your needs. Whether you need a business site, an online store, or a portfolio, we’ll help you figure it out.

Step 2: Design & Development

Our designers create a mockup tailored to your brand. Once approved, we build your website, ensuring it’s fast, responsive, and user-friendly.

Step 3: Hosting & Domain

We guide you in purchasing a domain name (yourbusiness.com) and hosting (the online server for your site). Don’t worry—we’ll handle the technical parts.

Step 4: Launch & Training

We launch your website and provide training so you can manage content. If you'd rather focus on your business, we offer maintenance packages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our services

What is a domain name and why do I need one?
A domain name is your website's unique address on the internet. It's what people type to find your website (e.g., yourbusiness.com). Without it, your website won't be accessible online.
What is web hosting and why do I need it?
Web hosting is the service that stores your website's files and makes them accessible on the internet. Without hosting, your website cannot be viewed online.
Can I use my own domain name?
Yes! If you already have a domain name, we can help you connect it to your website.
What is SEO, and why does my website need it?
SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization. It helps your website appear higher in search engine results, making it easier for customers to find you online.
How long does it take to build a website?
The time varies depending on the type and complexity of the website. Simple websites may take 1-2 weeks, while more complex projects can take 4-6 weeks or longer.
Will my website work on mobile devices?
Yes, all the websites we create are fully responsive, meaning they work seamlessly on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.
Can I update the website myself after it’s built?
Yes! We provide training so you can easily update your website. Alternatively, we offer maintenance packages if you'd prefer us to handle updates.
What is the cost of building a website?
The cost depends on the type and complexity of the website. Business websites start at €500, while e-commerce sites start at €1000. See the pricing section for more details.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes, we offer flexible payment plans to help make our services accessible to everyone.
Do you handle website maintenance?
Yes, we offer maintenance packages that include updates, backups, and performance monitoring.
Can I sell products on my website?
Yes, we create e-commerce websites with secure payment integrations and dynamic product catalogs.
Will my website be secure?
Absolutely. We implement SSL certificates and other security measures to protect your website and customer data.
Can I see the progress of my website during development?
Yes, we provide regular updates and previews throughout the development process.
What if I don’t like the design?
We collaborate closely with you during the design phase to ensure you're satisfied. Revisions are included in the process.
Do you write the content for my website?
Yes, we can provide professional content writing services if needed.
What is a CMS, and do I need one?
A CMS (Content Management System) allows you to manage your website content easily. We use popular CMS platforms like WordPress.
Do you offer refunds?
Refund policies vary by project. Contact us for more details if you have concerns.
