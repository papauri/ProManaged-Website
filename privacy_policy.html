Privacy Policy

The twelve sections below set out in full how ProManaged IT collects, uses, protects and shares personal information — and the rights you hold over it.

1. Introduction

ProManaged IT is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in our data handling practices. This comprehensive privacy policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

2. Information Collection and Usage

2.1 Personal Data We Collect

  • Contact information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Business information
  • Technical data (IP address, browser type, device information)
  • Usage data related to our IT services

2.2 Purpose of Collection

  • Service delivery and improvement
  • Customer support
  • Communication about services
  • Legal compliance and security

3. Data Protection Measures

We implement robust security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Data encryption
  • Access controls
  • Regular security assessments
  • Secure data storage systems

4. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request data correction
  • Withdraw consent
  • Request data deletion
  • Receive a copy of your data

5. Third-Party Sharing

We maintain strict controls over data sharing with third parties:

  • Service providers (with appropriate safeguards)
  • Legal requirements
  • Business partners (only with explicit consent)

6. Cookie Usage

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Improve website functionality
  • Analyze usage patterns
  • Enhance security
  • Provide personalized experience

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to — and maintained on — computers located outside of your state, province, country or other governmental jurisdiction where the data protection laws may differ from those of your jurisdiction.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Updates and Changes

This privacy policy is regularly reviewed and updated to reflect:

  • Changes in our practices
  • New regulatory requirements
  • Technology updates

11. Contact Information

For privacy-related inquiries:

  • Email: info@promanaged-it.com

12. Compliance Statement

This privacy policy complies with applicable data protection laws including GDPR, ensuring your data rights are protected according to international standards.
