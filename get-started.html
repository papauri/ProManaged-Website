What to bring

Five things that make the first call useful.

None of them are required. Every one of them saves a round trip.

The problem, not the solution

“Bookings get double-entered and we lose one a week” tells us far more than “we need an app”. Describe what actually goes wrong, and how often.

A rough budget

Even a range helps. It is not used to price up to your ceiling — it decides what is worth proposing at all, and whether we should tell you to spend it elsewhere.

Any real deadline

A season, an event, an office move. Real dates change the plan; invented urgency only changes the price.

What you use today

Spreadsheets, apps, paper, or a group chat that has quietly become a system. Whatever it is, it tells us how you work.

Where things need to land

For sourcing work especially — the delivery destination shapes cost, timing and what is realistic to order at all.

Pick a starting point

Not sure which one you need?

Choose whichever is closest and we will work the rest out on the call. Plenty of projects turn out to be two of these at once.
